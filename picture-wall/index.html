Boxes:

How to use

  • Copy from Google Images: right-click a picture → Copy image. Click a box here, then press Ctrl + V.
  • Drag: drag a picture from another tab straight onto a box.
  • From the computer: double-click an empty box to choose a file.
  • Change a picture: click the box with the old picture, then paste (Ctrl + V) or drag the new one onto it. It replaces the old one.
  • Make one picture big: double-click a box with a picture. Click again to go back.
  • Remove a picture: point at it and click ✕ (or click the box and press Delete).
  • Full screen: press ⛶ or F11. The top bar hides; move the mouse to the very top to show it.
  • Pictures stay even if you refresh the page (on this computer).
